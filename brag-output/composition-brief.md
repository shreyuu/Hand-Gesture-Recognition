# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Hand Gesture Recognition

## Objective
Create a short launch-style brag video for Hand Gesture Recognition.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape — 1920x1080
- Duration: 22.5 seconds

## Source Material
- Project root: `Hand-Gesture-Recognition/`
- Primary files read: `README.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `main.py`, `gesture_recognition/app.py`, `config.py`, `recorder.py`, `trainer.py`, `services/*`, `tracking/hand_detector.py`, `data/gesture.names`, `recorded_gestures/index_20260709_201708.json`
- Product name: Hand Gesture Recognition
- Tagline / strongest claim: real-time hand gesture recognition through a webcam, with voice feedback, and you can train your own gestures
- Key UI to recreate: the OpenCV window "Hand Gesture Recognition" — MediaPipe skeleton (red dots, light bones), red `label (confidence)` at top-left, green `FPS:` top-right, `Voice: ON/OFF` and the instruction line at the bottom
- Real data used: 20 of the 100 frames in `recorded_gestures/index_20260709_201708.json`, normalized (wrist-relative, scale = wrist→middle MCP) and played back as the hook skeleton. The other gesture poses are modeled 21-point skeletons in MediaPipe landmark order (`hand-data.js`, generated by a small pose script).
- Copy that must appear verbatim:
  - `Hand Gesture Recognition` (window title — `cv2.imshow` name)
  - `Press 'v' to toggle voice, 'r' to record, 'q' to quit`
  - `Voice: OFF` / `Voice: ON`
  - gesture names from `data/gesture.names`
  - `Saved 100 samples to recorded_gestures/index_20260709_201708.json`
  - `Model saved to models/custom_model`
  - `python main.py record --gesture index`, `python main.py train`, `python main.py recognize --model custom`

## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: default
- Creative direction: late-night webcam demo reel — the OpenCV debug window is the star
- Interpretation: playful but technical; heavy display type for claims, mono for real app strings; motion carried by the skeleton morphing on the beat.
- Angle: "Your hand is 21 dots." The skeleton is the continuous subject from the first frame to the last.
- Hook: 21 red dots pop in, bones connect, the real recording plays back — "Your hand is 21 dots."
- Outro / punchline: "Hand Gesture Recognition" → "Press 'q' to quit."
- Avoid: generic SaaS language, abstract filler, redesigning the OpenCV overlay into something it isn't.

- Audio role: warm bed + moderate motion-matched UI accents
- Music: `assets/music/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, 0.32, fade out from 21.3s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset (`happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`, 120.19 BPM). Beat-grid morphs at 7.02/8.02/9.02/10.02/11.02; terminal Enters at 16.02/17.02/18.02; beat-locked punchline at strong cue 20.02.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — bass band drives the red glow behind the hand (scale/opacity). Data pre-extracted with hyperframes-creative `extract-audio-data.py` into `audio-data.js`.
- SFX: soft rollover ticks + drop on dots/bones, soft impact on window reveal, soft clicks per gesture, keypress + switch for `v`, drop for speech bubble, sparse key ticks for typing, click per Enter, warm bell on title, click on punchline. All from `sfx-analysis.md` low/medium-risk picks, 0.35–0.7 volume.
